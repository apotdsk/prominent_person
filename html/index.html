The image of the Star Wars character Darth Vader appeared in Ukrainian politics in November 2012. “Darth Vader” became the head of the ”Internet Party of Ukraine”

The idea was born out of a joking remark by one of Odesa's politicians that corruption in the city council is so bad that even Darth Vader would be given a state land certificate there. On 14 February 2011, a man dressed as Darth Vader applied to the Odesa City Council for a free 10 hectares of land. According to him, he found out that the city council was allocating land on the coastal slopes for free, and he came to get a plot for himself, justifying it as follows:

'I know that the city council members, the executive committee and Mayor Kostusev have gone over to the Dark Side of the Force and will therefore help me.'

The rise of Political Activity

  • In September 2015, Darth Vader was nominated as a candidate for mayor of Odesa
